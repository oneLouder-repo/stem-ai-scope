# Executive summary: the state of play, November 2026

*STEM AI Scope · scan window May 11 – November 15, 2026 · Canada first, UK/EU second*

## The headline

Canada now has a national AI strategy that names youth AI literacy as a priority, plus the money to deliver it at scale. Educators and equity-seeking communities are asking who it is built for and how safe it is. For a national youth STEM charity, the opening is real, but it rewards organisations that can show they are reaching underserved youth and teaching *critical* AI literacy, not just tool use.

## What changed in six months

- **Policy and money arrived together.** *AI for All* launched on June 4 with no standalone AI law. It relies instead on privacy and online-safety bills ([Geist](https://www.michaelgeist.ca/2026/06/ai-for-all-details-to-follow-government-releases-a-big-spending-ai-strategy-that-is-still-short-on-the-specifics-that-matter/)). CanCode's fifth phase put [$30M toward 1.1 million students and 76,000 teachers](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/07/government-of-canada-invests-30-million-to-launch-fifth-phase-of-cancode-program-to-offer-new-training-opportunities-for-youth-and-teachers.html). In September the [National AI Literacy Initiative](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/09/government-of-canada-launches-national-ai-literacy-initiative.html), led by Amii, began targeting one million post-secondary learners and 50,000+ K-12 educators.
- **The national institutes became literacy providers.** Amii, Mila and Vector now run teacher kits, instructor curricula, youth programs and public courses alongside their research.
- **Teachers pushed back.** The Canadian Teachers' Federation says the initiative puts [adoption before safety](https://www.educationnewscanada.com/article/education/level/k12/3/1223177/canadian-teachers-federation-concerned-national-ai-literacy-initiative-puts-adoption-before-safety.html). Surveys show near-universal student use of AI and teacher worry about critical thinking ([Fraser/Leger](https://thehub.ca/2026/08/28/canadian-teachers-say-ai-is-doing-students-homework-and-their-thinking/), [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ca/en/media/2026/09/majority-of-students-use-ai-but-educators-sound-alarm.html)).
- **Indigenous AI moved into the core of the ecosystem.** Mila and CIFAR launched an [Indigenous AI Advisory Circle](https://mila.quebec/en/news/mila-and-cifar-launch-the-indigenous-ai-advisory-circle). FLAIR's Indigenous-language work was named in the strategy, and editorials pressed on data and resource sovereignty.
- **Black communities are organising around exclusion.** Critics noted that no Black members sat on the strategy task force. New community programs include Amii's work with young Black founders and a [$135.7M Black Canadian Communities Program](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/news/2026/09/government-of-canada-launches-black-canadian-communities-program.html).
- **Europe set the reference points.** The EU–OECD [AI Literacy Framework](https://education.ec.europa.eu/node/3537) (19 competences) and a planned EU AI-literacy certificate for 15-year-olds give a ready benchmark for curriculum design.

## What it means for Actua

1. **Position Actua as the youth-facing partner of the national literacy push.** The federal initiative mainly targets post-secondary learners and teachers, which leaves out-of-school youth largely unserved.
2. **Lead with critical, culturally grounded AI literacy.** That answers teachers' safety concerns and fits Actua's Indigenous, girls and rural/remote mandate.
3. **Map programs to the AILit framework and UNESCO competencies.** This makes impact measurable, which is the gap the CTF identified.
4. **Partner on Indigenous-led and Black-led AI** (Indigenous Pathfinders, FLAIR, ACCEC, Ethos Lab) under OCAP and data-sovereignty principles, rather than building in parallel.
5. **Use creative and futurist methods as an advantage.** Afrofuturist and Indigenous-futurist speculative design is showing up in research and the arts as an effective way into critical AI literacy for youth.

## Watch next

Budget 2026 implementation, rollout metrics for the literacy initiative, provincial K-12 AI guidance, the CanCode phase-five awards, and the EU certificate pilots.
